Why bother?

Loads of people are going through stuff and not saying anything.

Mostly because they think everyone else has it figured out. They don't.

Open Up Day is our way of making it normal to talk about it. You can learn a few ways to deal with stress, meet the people who do this for a living (they're nice), and see that you're not the only one.

It's for teens and young adults, around 14 to 25. Parents, teachers and coaches are welcome too. Bring a friend if that makes it easier.

What's on

Wander around, pop in for a bit or stay till the end. Nobody's going to make you share anything.

Chill out zone

Beanbags, quiet music and nobody asking questions. Come here if it gets to be a lot.

Try stuff

Breathing, journaling, drawing, mindfulness. Quick things you can use on a bad day.

Find help

Kind words wall

Ask us anything

Myth or not?

A few things people believe that just aren't true.

Myth"Only weak people ask for help."
ActuallyIt takes guts. Most people who reach out say they wish they'd done it sooner.
Myth"It's not serious enough to talk about."
ActuallyIf it's bugging you, it counts. You don't need to be at breaking point.
Myth"Everyone else is fine."
ActuallyAbout 1 in 5 young people deal with a mental health issue in a given year. You're in good company.
